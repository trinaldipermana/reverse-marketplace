# Product showcase brief

Fill in what is known. Mark each demo step as rehearsed, planned, or out of scope. Label details that are private or need approval before sharing.

- **Audience and goal** — Who is in the room? What single action should they take afterward?
- **Problem and users** — Who experiences the problem? What do they do today, and where does it break down?
- **Product and positioning** — What do buyers and sellers each do? What changes for them?
- **Current stage and demo** — What has been rehearsed? List the sequence, environment, mock components, and a fallback if available.
- **Evidence and limits** — Link each approved claim to a spec section, demo recording, test result, interview, or other supporting source. Include dates and scope for any numbers.
- **Next milestone** — What is the team testing next? For a partner, what would each side contribute and what would the pilot measure?
