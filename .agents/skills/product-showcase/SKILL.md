# Product showcase

Prepare materials the presenter can review and share. Use the requested audience and format. For a full package, draft a one-page overview, a roughly 90-second walkthrough, and a short follow-up with one call to action.

## Gather what matters

- Read the root `INITIAL_SPEC.md` and any supplied brief, demo notes, and relevant artifacts. The specification describes intended behavior. Use the current repo and evidence from the presenter to determine what can actually be shown.
- If there is no brief, use the [product brief template](assets/product-brief.md) to identify gaps. Ask only for facts that would change the pitch, especially the target audience, product mechanics, runnable demo steps, or the desired next action. If a core fact is missing, ask before drafting claims that depend on it. If only the call to action is missing, offer one as a draft and flag it for confirmation.
- Keep three kinds of claims distinct. Use demonstrated capabilities with their scope and environment, describe planned features as proposals, and leave unsupported claims out of shareable copy. A working mock flow shows how the prototype behaves. Customer usage, commercial integrations, real payments, and traction each need their own evidence. When sources disagree, flag the conflict privately and use only the claims they support together.

## Write for the audience

- **Investor:** Connect the buyer or seller problem to the product mechanism and the most credible evidence available. State the next milestone if supplied. Use the requested ask or suggest a follow-up conversation.
- **Partner:** Show the overlap with the partner's audience or workflow, each side's proposed contribution, and what a small pilot could test. Treat unconfirmed responsibilities and commitments as proposals.
- **Live walkthrough:** Give the presenter screen or action cues. For Reverse Marketplace, the spec's seeded Jakarta laptop request offers a concrete story. Use the confirmed sequence for live beats. Describe unconfirmed beats as rehearsal targets in presenter notes. Identify the mock environment and payment in the talk track, and show a seller policy limit if the exception path is ready.

Return a separate **Private review checklist** mapping material claims to their source and status, with any unresolved evidence gaps or proposed call to action. Before returning the package, check the overview, script, and follow-up against the checklist. Remove any claim or live cue flagged for confirmation, or describe it as a proposal where appropriate. Keep confidential material out of shareable copy. Draft in chat unless the user requests a file. Leave sending and publishing to the presenter.
